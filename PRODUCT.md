# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, confirmed by the user.

## Users

Pet owners organizing their animals' health records, and veterinary clinics or professionals who need to register care and share history with owners.

## Product Purpose

Cloudpet keeps vaccination records, exams, prescriptions, appointments, and reminders together in the cloud so pet care is easier to follow and share.

## Positioning

One connected history that follows the pet between the owner's pocket and the clinic's dashboard.

## Operating Context

Owners use the product on mobile or desktop. Clinics and veterinarians use a dashboard to register care and share records. Existing paper records can be photographed and added.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Digital vaccination card with doses, dates, and boosters.
- Storage for exams, prescriptions, and reports.
- Appointments and reminders for consultations, grooming, and vaccines.
- Shared history between owner and clinic.
- No verified production signup URL, email, Instagram handle, customer evidence, or commercial claims were supplied; the existing placeholders remain explicit.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is cloudpet. The supplied Figma design and its assets are binding visual references. The confirmed voice is warm, direct, and Brazilian Portuguese, including “Menos papelada. Mais patinhas.”

## Evidence on Hand

The Figma file supplies product copy, logo, pet imagery, and product-interface mockups. No testimonials, customer logos, metrics, or external proof were supplied and none should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Keep the pet's health information easy to find.
- Connect owners and clinics around one shared history.
- Reduce paperwork without making care feel impersonal.
- Make the next action clear on both mobile and desktop.
